Understanding Context Usage

What is Context?

Context is the AI's "working memory" for your conversation. It includes your messages, the AI's responses, and relevant document content. Lana AI uses a 32,000 token context window (approximately 24,000 words) per conversation.

Context Breakdown

  • • System Instructions: Base AI personality and capabilities
  • • Conversation History: Your messages and AI responses
  • • Document Content: Relevant sections from uploaded files

Why Does It Show 100% Available?

New Conversation

When you start a new chat, 100% of the context window is available since there's no message history yet.

After Auto-Compact

When conversations get long, Lana automatically summarizes older messages to free up space, restoring most of your context.

What is "85% Until Auto-Compact"?

When your conversation uses 85% of the context window (about 27,200 tokens), Lana automatically compacts the conversation by:

  1. Summarizing older messages into concise summaries
  2. Preserving recent messages (last 5-10 exchanges) in full
  3. Maintaining important context and document references
  4. Freeing up 50-70% of the context window for new messages

Note: Auto-compact happens automatically and seamlessly. You'll see a brief notification, but the conversation continues without interruption.
