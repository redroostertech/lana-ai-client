What Are Management Boards?

Management Boards are your organization's performance dashboard. They provide a structured way to track Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), monitor team metrics, and measure what matters most to your practice across intake, caseload, revenue, and custom areas.

Why Use Management Boards?

Centralized Metrics

All your KPIs in one place - track performance across intake, caseload, revenue, and custom metrics without scattered spreadsheets.

Scheduled Reviews

Set review frequencies (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual) and track submission deadlines to ensure consistent monitoring.

Team Accountability

Assign metrics to specific team members for submission, creating clear ownership and accountability for each KPI.

Data-Driven Insights

Make informed decisions with historical data, trends, and submission tracking across all your organization's key metrics.

Board Types

Intake Board

Track lead sources, consultations scheduled, conversion rates, and initial contact metrics to optimize your client acquisition funnel.

Caseload Board

Monitor active cases, workload distribution, case resolution time, and individual attorney productivity to balance team capacity.

Revenue Board

Track revenue targets, billable hours, collection rates, and financial performance to ensure your practice meets its goals.

Custom Board

Create boards for any metrics unique to your practice - marketing ROI, client satisfaction, staff training, or operational efficiency.

How Management Boards Work

1

Create a Board

Choose a board type (Intake, Caseload, Revenue, or Custom), give it a name, and set the review frequency that matches your management cycle.

2

Define Metrics

Add specific KPIs to track - define the metric name, type (number, percentage, currency, boolean), frequency, and assign team members responsible for submitting data.

3

Submit Values

Team members submit metric values according to the schedule. Each submission can include notes for context and is timestamped for historical tracking.

4

Review & Analyze

View submissions, track trends, and make data-driven decisions. Admins can approve submissions and monitor who's submitted versus who still needs to.

Pro Tips for Success

  • Start Small: Begin with 3-5 critical metrics per board and expand as you establish the habit
  • Clear Ownership: Assign each metric to a specific person to ensure accountability
  • Regular Reviews: Schedule team meetings to review board metrics and discuss trends
  • Actionable Metrics: Focus on metrics you can actually influence with decisions and actions
