What is this?

The Activity Overview is a calendar-style chart that shows when your organization was active over the last 90 days. Each small square represents one day; the color shows how much activity happened that day.

Why does it matter?

It helps you spot patterns at a glance: which days of the week are busiest, whether activity is steady or concentrated in certain weeks, and how engagement has changed over time.

How we track activity

Activity is counted from usage across your whole organization. Each time someone in your org uses LANA (conversations, documents, searches, and other interactions), that counts as activity. The chart adds up all of these actions per day.

The numbers you see are total activity events for that day, not unique users.

What am I looking at?

  • Rows. Each row is a day of the week (Sunday at the top through Saturday at the bottom).
  • Columns. Each column is one week. Time runs left to right.
  • Colors. Each square's color shows how much activity happened that day.

Color scale

  • Light gray: No activity (0 events)
  • Light green: Low activity (1 to 4 events)
  • Medium green: Moderate activity (5 to 9 events)
  • Dark green: High activity (10 to 14 events)
  • Darkest green: Very high activity (15 or more events)
