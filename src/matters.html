What Are Matters?

        
      


      
      

        
        

          

            Matters are the organizational foundation of your Lana AI workspace. They provide dedicated, isolated spaces for managing cases, projects, clients, or any work that requires separate context and privacy.
          

        


        
        

          
Why Use Matters?

          

            

              

                

                  
                

                

                  
Complete Isolation

                  
Each matter maintains its own documents, conversations, and AI context - no cross-contamination between cases.

                

              

            


            

              

                

                  
                

                

                  
Organized Workspace

                  
Keep related work together - all documents, chats, and research for a case in one dedicated space.

                

              

            


            

              

                

                  
                

                

                  
Contextual AI

                  
Lana AI learns and remembers each matter's unique context, providing more relevant and accurate assistance.

                

              

            


            

              

                

                  
                

                

                  
Team Collaboration

                  
Control access and permissions per matter, enabling secure collaboration with specific team members.

                

              

            

          

        


        
        

          
How Matters Work

          

            

              
1

              

                
Create a Matter

                
Start a new matter for each case, project, or client. Give it a descriptive name and optional details like matter ID, client name, and description.

              

            


            

              
2

              

                
Upload Documents

                
Add all relevant files to the matter's document library. Lana AI processes and indexes them for intelligent search and retrieval.

              

            


            

              
3

              

                
Start Conversations

                
Chat with Lana AI in the context of the matter. The AI has access to all matter documents and previous conversations for informed responses.

              

            


            

              
4

              

                
Manage & Organize

                
Track matter status (active, closed, archived), search across all content, and manage team access as your work progresses.

              

            

          

        


        
        

          
Best Practices

          

            
    
              
  • 
                
                One matter per case/project - Keep work isolated for clarity and privacy
              
    • 
              
  • 
                
                Use descriptive names - Make matters easy to find and identify later
              
    • 
              
  • 
                
                Upload key documents early - Give Lana AI full context from the start
              
    • 
              
  • 
                
                Archive completed matters - Keep your workspace focused on active work
              
    •
